Which logo direction fits Risen Development Solutions?

1Dawn Brackets

A sun rising between code brackets. Dev-literal and warm; works as an icon and as a wordmark lockup.

2Rising R

A geometric R built from ascending bars, like a growth chart. Sharp and corporate; scales down well as an app or avatar icon.

3Summit Chevrons

Stacked upward chevrons forming a peak, in a gradient. Bold and modern; reads as progress, elevation and ambition.

4Terminal Rise

Monospace wordmark with a blinking cursor and a sun-dot over the i. Hacker-persona energy; great for GitHub and a dev portfolio.
